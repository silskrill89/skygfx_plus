## Description
In this demo I explore 2d GI. My long-term goal was to find a way to calculate  light from faraway occluders in lower resolution than nearby occluders, this approach naturally produces soft shadows with natural penumbras.

For Path of Exile I have implemented a point light shadows algorithm that has that property, but making a GI algorithm using the same idea is much harder. That's why I decided to test drive it on a 2d test case first.

Even though it looks like there's only one light in this scene, actually each pixel can occlude, emit and receive light and the runtime cost is constant. I will demonstrate it in further demos.
