### 2. See-through / inside-out: reported depth + cull/overdraw defect, independent of the white

- The tester's “back of the face drawn on front” is exactly what a **CULLFRONT stencil-shadow volume** looks like when its color writes leak into the main pass. `pipelinecommon.cpp:1187–1191` documents the game's real `CULLFRONT` use: **ped stencil-shadow volumes at exe 0x70A9C3**. A volume drawn with `CULLFRONT`, no ZWRITE, and the shadow contract's canonical **white stage-0 texture** (main.cpp shadow-contract comments) would be a white, see-through, back-facing ped hull that follows CJ/vehicles and is the likely “ring/blob”.

- **Ground:** no confirmed see-through patch. The `yts` pavers, `Vf2` steps/road, `8GG` crosswalk, `wud` road, `bef` road, `gA5` tarmac are opaque. The `bef` dark triangle is a **flat shadow** (75,68,52 σ 0.4), and `Sar`'s ghosted ground is the **cloud/rotor overlay above it** (overlay 89,76,66 vs clear 30,24,18). `yN4` is a flat pink dawn surface (σ 0.0) — untextured-looking but opaque; not a hole.
- **Clouds/sprites:** Kze/jMZ pink flat panels (σ 0.1–0.6), bUj milky sheet with a jagged alpha edge, e1p flat beige polygons (σ 0.5), bef white sheet (σ 1.3). These are **alpha-class/sprite/blend** failures (flat color, hard polygon/alpha edges) and are **separate from the ped issue**. They do not follow CJ on foot.

## Ring observations

**No frame in this 20-shot set shows the ring/blob on the ground around CJ.** I checked the raw frames and gamma-lifted versions of every on-foot frame (yts, vOv, Eyl, 8GG, EOs, wud, bef, gA5, jQb); there is no circular boundary, no ring edge, no measurable radius, and no sharp-vs-ragged edge to report. The tester's ring is **not captured** here.

What the frames *do* support:
- **Best interpretation:** the ring is the **shadow/light boundary that follows CJ** — most likely the **player's real-time shadow** (or the shadow-map's lit/shadow edge), not a world-geometry sphere. `bef` is the direct control: `pDirect=0.996` but the CJ inside the bridge shadow is **dark** (58,50,29), i.e. shadow wins over the global light. `jQb` (shaded peds 53,49,46) and the dim `vOv`/`Eyl` controls (textured peds) agree; the direct-lit `yts`/`8GG`/`EOs`/`gA5` peds are white. The **chest silhouette** in `yts` is a projected shadow of a hidden ped/geometry, so the shadow system is demonstrably projecting characters onto CJ; the ring is the same light/shadow boundary at ground level. Inside the shadow the peds are **correct/dark/textured**; outside they are **white/clipped** — exactly the tester's statement. The “ring” itself may be the **normal player shadow**, noticed only because it is the boundary between correct and blown-out peds.
- **Alternative:** the ring is a **stencil-shadow volume** silhouette (CULLFRONT) or a **shadow-map coverage circle**. That would also follow CJ and would explain the EOs van disc (a volume silhouette intersecting the van) and the reported front/back inversion. It does not by itself explain the pDirect control pair.
- **Do not conflate:** the Kze/jMZ pink sky panels, Sar/bUj/e1p milky sheets, and 2KU rotor blur are **cloud/rotor sprites at altitude**, not the CJ ring. The EOs van disc is the only world-space “sphere” in the set.

To close this item, capture a frame with the ring visible and a ped **straddling** it, plus a second frame with the ring at a known distance from CJ. Without that, shape/radius/edge sharpness are **not measurable**.

## What would confirm / kill each

- **White = over-exposure (confirm):** force `pDirect` for the skin/ped pass to the timecycle night value (or multiply by `dirMult`/night balance) at 01:56/02:03; if the white peds become blue/brown like `vOv`/`Eyl`, confirmed. **Shadow-boundary confirm:** the existing `bef` control already shows `pDirect=0.996` with a shadowed CJ **dark**; if a single frame shows a lit ped white and a shadowed ped dark, the boundary is light/shadow, not texture. **Kill:** if peds stay white with `pDirect≈0.0`/direct-light disabled, or a shadowed `bef`-type CJ is also white, the white is a shadow-volume/overdraw (or missing texture, already unlikely).
- **White = shadow-volume overdraw (confirm):** set `pedShadows=0` (and/or `stencilShadows=0`, disable the ped stencil-volume path at 0x70A9C3); if the white hulls and the ring vanish while the real peds remain, confirmed. RenderDoc: the white draw should be a **second** draw of the same geometry after the textured ped, with `CULLFRONT`, ZWRITE off, and the white 1×1 texture. **Kill:** if the only ped draw is the textured one and the white is the same draw, it is lighting.
- **See-through = ZWRITE (confirm):** log the **per-draw** ZWRITE/ZFUNC/cull (not entry/exit) and the ped depth; force ZWRITE=1 on every ped draw and `dualPassPed=0`; if the wrong inter-ped overlap/staircase disappears, confirmed. **Kill:** if the overlap survives with ZWRITE=1, it is **alpha blend** or a second pass/volume; check blend and draw count.
