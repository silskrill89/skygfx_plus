## Visual Target: LA 1992 Film Photography
- Warm color temperature (slightly orange/yellow)
- Film grain present
- Slight vignetting
- Deep shadows with warm fill
- Sky: warm gradient, not pure blue
- Skin: warm tones, slight orange bias
